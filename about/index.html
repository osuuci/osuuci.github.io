About

osu! UCI was founded in 2014 by Max Chung at the University of California, Irvine, and remains highly active to this day. We are a social and gaming club built around playing and discussing osu!, a free-to-win, occasionally frustrating, but always fun rhythm game.

Although we are affliated with the University of California, Irvine, any osu! player is free to hang out with us online and offline. All players are welcome, no matter your skill level. In fact, even if you don't play osu! at all, you are still welcome to join us. If interested, join our Discord for all news and announcements. Be on the lookout for our weekly meetings and our winter and spring LANs!

Meetings

Our weekly in-person meetings take place Fridays at 6 PM at Donald Bren Hall. See our Discord for details. Possible meeting activities include:

  • osu! multi's
  • Board games
  • Group web games
  • Round1 Arcade (transportation provided)
  • Karaoke
  • osu! mapping workshops
  • osu! battle royale
  • Sporting events
  • Hiking
  • Potluck and movie
